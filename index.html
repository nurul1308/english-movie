🎞️ Mission briefing

Filem contoh: The Secret Island

Genre: Adventure

Setting: A mysterious island

Story: Three friends find a map and solve clues to save a village.

Opinion: Exciting, funny and full of surprises.

Your mission: explain the genre, setting, story, favourite character, opinion and recommendation.

🗣️ 3. Speaking Studio

Pilih satu soalan, rancang jawapan dan bercakap selama 30–60 saat.

Sentence starters

• The film I want to talk about is…

• It is a/an … film.

• The story is about…

• My favourite character is… because…

• I enjoyed / did not enjoy it because…

• I recommend this film to… because…

Sediakan sekurang-kurangnya 3 idea sebelum bercakap.

✍️ 4. Movie Review Workshop

Review checklist

🎬 Title and genre • 📍 Setting • 📖 Main story • ⭐ Opinion • 👍 Recommendation

Contoh frasa: “The film is about…”, “The acting was…”, “I would recommend it to…”

Senarai semak ulasan akan dipaparkan di sini.
Model review (example)

The Secret Island is an adventure film about three friends who discover a mysterious map. The story takes place on a beautiful island. The friends follow clues and try to save a village. I enjoyed the film because it was exciting and funny. The actors were good, and the ending was surprising. My favourite character was Leo because he was brave and helpful. I recommend this film to teenagers who enjoy adventure stories.

🏆 Final Challenge: Reel Star

Gunakan soalan ini untuk pembentangan lisan selama 1 minit:

Talk about a film you enjoyed.

You should say:

• what the film is called and its genre

• where and when the story takes place

• what happens in the story

• why you like or dislike it

• whether you recommend it and why
